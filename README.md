# DRIPLAB — streetwear designer

Pick a product (T-shirt, hoodie, joggers, cap, sneaker, tote) or upload your own, then draw and design on it.

**Run it:** open `index.html` in a browser. No build step, no server, no dependencies (Google Fonts load if online).

## Features
- **Products:** 6 built-in silhouettes with front/back (sneaker: outer/inner), 14 garment colors + custom picker. Your artwork carries over when you switch products.
- **Your own product:** upload a photo/PNG; white backgrounds are removed automatically and artwork is clipped to the product's shape.
- **Tools:** brush, spray paint, eraser, fill bucket (respects seams/pockets), line, rectangle, ellipse, color picker, mirror-symmetry drawing.
- **Text:** 9 streetwear fonts (gothic, marker, pixel, etc.), outline, arc/curved text, letter spacing.
- **Images & stickers:** add logos/images (button or drag-and-drop), 24 emoji stickers. Move, scale, rotate, flip, layer order, opacity, duplicate.
- **Undo/redo** per side, autosave of the current session, **save designs** to a local gallery (browser storage).
- **Export:** mockup PNG (one side or all sides) and a transparent **print file** with the artwork only.

## Shortcuts
V move · B brush · S spray · E eraser · G fill · L line · R rect · O ellipse · T text · I picker · M mirror · `[` `]` size · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo/redo · Ctrl+S save · Ctrl+D duplicate · Delete removes selected · arrows nudge
